What does a crawler cost your team?

Put in your seats and years. See Screaming Frog's public list price next to Toadstone, our own free tool, with the caveats that matter.

Read this first. Toadstone is our own tool, and this page compares it with one paid competitor, so treat it as our view. It does only arithmetic on Screaming Frog's public list price, which we read from their pricing page on 2026-10-24[S1]. It does not claim Toadstone is better, only that its licence costs nothing. We are not affiliated with Screaming Frog. See the full comparison, including what Toadstone doesn't do.

Might you not need to pay at all? Screaming Frog's free version crawls up to 500 URLs[S1]. If your sites are smaller than that, the free version may be all you need, and so may Toadstone.

What this calculator does not count

  • Toadstone is not free to run. It is free to license, but you host it (Docker, or Python 3.11+ with Chromium)[T1], and someone has to look after it. The hosting field above lets you add that cost. Your own time is not counted anywhere.
  • No support contract. Screaming Frog lists technical support with a licence[S1]. Toadstone has none.
  • Renewals. Licences last one year[S1][S4]. We assume you renew at the same list price. We could not confirm a separate renewal rate, so your real price may differ.
  • Tiers. The per-licence price drops when you buy 5, 10 or 20 or more licences at the same time[S1]. We assume one purchase for all seats.
  • Features. Cost is only one axis. The comparison lists where Screaming Frog is ahead, including crawl scale and a desktop app.
  • Taxes, discounts, add-on products (such as their Log File Analyser) and promotions are not included.
